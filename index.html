Dreams Don't Need Permission

Project Overview

Dreams Don't Need Permission is a five-module inspirational microlearning series designed to help learners overcome self-doubt, build confidence, develop resilience, and take meaningful action toward personal and professional goals.

Developed in Articulate Rise 360, the series leverages microlearning principles, reflective learning activities, and behavior-focused design to encourage mindset transformation and sustained action.

Learning Objectives

  • Recognize limiting beliefs and self-imposed barriers
  • Develop a growth-oriented mindset
  • Build confidence through intentional action
  • Strengthen resilience when facing setbacks
  • Create a personal action plan for meaningful goals

Modules

  • Module 1 – Dreams Don't Need Permission
  • Module 2 – Identifying Self-Doubt and Limiting Beliefs
  • Module 3 – Building Confidence Through Action
  • Module 4 – Overcoming Fear of Failure and Rejection
  • Module 5 – Creating a Personal Commitment to Action
